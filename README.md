### Open-Science & Research // Library of Alexandria

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="100%">
      <div align="left">
        <h3>Library of Alexandria — Scientific Evidence Grading</h3>
        <p>
          An open-source scientific knowledge platform powered by autonomous research workers. Queries primary literature (<strong>Europe PMC, OpenAlex</strong>) and clinical trial registries (<strong>ClinicalTrials.gov</strong>) to extract concrete claims and deterministically compute evidence grades (<code>T1</code>–<code>T6</code>) based on study design and sample size.
        </p>
        <p>
          <em>Frontier and minority hypotheses remain visible and clearly graded, avoiding silent filtering while letting the evidence speak for itself.</em>
        </p>
